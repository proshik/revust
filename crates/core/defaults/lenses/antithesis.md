## Case against the proposal

Investigate whether the proposed change solves the actual problem and what regressions it could introduce. Identify evidence that would disprove its assumptions and distinguish a weak proposal from an invalid issue.

Challenge both the reported failure and the proposed remedy against actual consistency, recovery and compatibility guarantees. Could existing protections exclude the trigger? Could the remedy introduce new failure modes or rollout hazards? Do not dismiss a valid issue merely because its proposed solution is excessive.
