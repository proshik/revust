## Case against the proposal

Investigate whether the proposed change solves the actual problem and what regressions it could introduce. Identify evidence that would disprove its assumptions and distinguish a weak proposal from an invalid issue.

Challenge both the reported failure and the proposed remedy using actual isolation, delivery, acknowledgement and compatibility guarantees. Could existing constraints or recovery exclude the trigger? Could an outbox, new dependency or migration introduce duplicates, ordering changes or rollout hazards? Do not dismiss a valid issue merely because its proposed solution is excessive.
