## Cost and alternatives

Compare leaving the behavior alone, a narrow correction and the proposed intervention. Consider migration, operational burden and ongoing maintenance. Ground cost claims in identifiable work rather than invented estimates.

Where needed, compare reuse of existing mechanisms, an appropriate library and a local correction against the required guarantee. Include compatibility, data migration, rollout/rollback, backlog/cleanup, replay and operational ownership in identifiable intervention costs. Do not prescribe an outbox, dependency or redesign when current guarantees already meet the contract.
