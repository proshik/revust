## Issue evidence

Locate the behavior described by the issue in the current code and available artifacts. Separate reproducible facts from untested assumptions and identify the information needed to settle uncertainty.

Establish the required guarantee, supported versions and affected operation before inferring a defect. Trace transaction/commit/publication and consumer-effect/ack boundaries with concrete code or artifacts, including actual driver and broker semantics. Separate contradictory documentation, missing operational guidance and untested assumptions; identify the precise evidence needed to settle each.
